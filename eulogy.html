Mum's Eulogy

  • I've known my mum for 48 years of my life, but in these last few days, I learnt some things about her that I didn't know.
  • I knew she was a good and loving soul, but not to the extent she touched the lives of other people.
  • Throughout her wake I witnessed small acts of connection that moved me.
—
  • For example, every time we gathered for family dinners at my mum's place on Fridays her next door Indian neighbour Lakshmi would always gift us some food.
  • When I thanked Lakshmi on the 1st day of the wake I learnt that whenever mum went marketing she would ask Lakshmi if she needed anything and would buy it for her.
  • Another thing I found out from the Presbyterian eldercare centre staff and residents is that my mum was always giving away her beloved plants to people.
  • It was on the 2nd day of the wake that I met David, which was the son of Ah-Loy (one of the residents), who dropped by to give his condolences because his mum and my mum were close.
  • It is through her green babies that my mum got to know Christian church members from the nearby Chapel of the Ascension.
  • They came by as a cell-group to spread the good word door-to-door, chanced upon my mum's beautiful corridor of plants and chatted about them, and then became friends that visited quite often.
—
  • I really loved all the stories told by mum's ex-colleagues and students.
  • One of her ex-students, Melody (who must have been in her 30s), dropped by after seeing the wake details in her alumni chat group.
  • My mum retired about 20 years ago, having spent the majority of it as an art teacher at First Toa Payoh Secondary School.
  • Melody remembered my mum as a dedicated art teacher, whom she painted the backdrop murals of the school with.
  • Melody become a designer, and she attributed this to having been influenced by my mum.
—
  • I was touched by the stories told by aunty Ah Pong and Chor Hiang (her ex-student who became her colleague), and numerous other ex-colleagues from her school.
  • They remembered her as a teacher who "talks very fast and walks very fast", and was a foundational pillar of the school who taught there for close to 40 years.
  • I learnt that my mum hand-drew artwork for the Normal Technical exams (there was no template then and this was beyond what was requied), and she really loved teaching art.
  • Besides normal canvas painting, she did pyrography, embroidery, calligraphy, batik, pottery, basket weaving and more with her students.
  • She helped create the school murals, and designed the school magazine covers.
  • They remembered my mum as a colleague and friend who loved plants and could name the varieties of the plants in school.
  • She was a strict but fair discipline mistress that was always vivacious and full of life.
  • From ex-students, to ex-colleagues, to her numerous nieces and nephews, to neighbours and church and center friends, my mum touched the lives of people around her.
—
  • In summary: She lived a full life, filled with joy and laughter.
  • She put me and my sis through school, was a terror of a tiger mum (to me, my sis and also the grandkids), and was a fierce righteous person that brooked no nonsense.
  • She loved her plants and her craftwork, and was always constantly doing projects.
  • She loved to travel, and visited many places in her life.
  • She had her flaws, but they paled in comparison to her virtues: She was a generous and loving soul who took all the hard work upon herself to make sure the people she loved was okay.
  • She helped shape who I am today. My wedding, which was supposed to be this year, was moved forward last year for mum.
—
  • It is my honor to be her son and I'm grateful for the time we had together.
  • I am most thankful that her death was peaceful and painless even through the late stages of cancer.
  • Mum, I wish you had more time to spend with my wife, but it is good enough.
  • I wish your life these past few years was sometimes not so hard, but you also had joy and love, and it is good enough.
  • Thanks mum for everything. You are loved and will be missed.
